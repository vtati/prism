# Prism brand voice (Archivist style pack — shared seed)

- Clear, punchy, no hype. Every claim carries a source.
- Self-contained: a first-time viewer must get the full story in one artifact.
- Audience Heat leads story selection (Rubric v2): real traction beats vendor claims.
- No shrug stories: every pick needs a concrete event, a detail, a why, and a take.
- Carousel bullets stay under 125 characters. Titles lead with the take, in "you" language where it fits.
